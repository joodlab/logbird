Compare
How Logbird compares.
There's more than one good way to keep an audit trail. These pages compare Logbird with other tools, using only facts we could check in their official docs, pricing pages and repositories. Each page says where the other tool is the better fit. We checked every fact about the other tools on 7 November 2026 and list every source. If something has changed, tell us and we'll fix it.
Which tool fits
Each tool answers a different question.
Retraced
An open-source (Apache-2.0) audit log service with an embeddable React viewer. Self-hosted.
Fits when: You want to embed an audit log viewer in your own React app, in a self-hosted Apache-2.0 service.
Logbird and Retraced compared
WorkOS Audit Logs
A hosted audit log API with an Admin Portal viewer for your customers' IT admins, and streaming to SIEM tools.
Fits when: Your enterprise customers' IT admins need a ready-made portal and SIEM streams today, and you'd rather not run any infrastructure.
Logbird and WorkOS Audit Logs compared
Pangea Secure Audit Log
A hosted, tamperproof audit log with cryptographic proofs. Part of CrowdStrike since September 2025.
Fits when: You need cryptographic proof that audit events haven't been changed.
Logbird and Pangea Secure Audit Log compared
Bemi
Different category
A different category: automatic change capture for Postgres. Records every row change without instrumenting your code.
Fits when: You want every Postgres row change captured automatically, without instrumenting your code.
Logbird and Bemi compared
Logbird
Fits when: your events must stay on your own servers, you want to send any event shape, and your team needs an operator console to answer "what happened?".
