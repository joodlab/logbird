Pricing
Self-hosting will be free. Cloud pricing isn’t set yet.
Logbird is MIT-licensed, and every feature in the repository will be yours to run once the source opens. Logbird Cloud runs the same codebase for you, in early access; its pricing will be published before billing starts.

Self-hosted
What you get when you self-host.
Every feature below is in the repository today, under the MIT licence.
Ingest over HTTP or the TypeScript SDK
One event per action; only action is required.
Search
The query language, or a guarded SQL WHERE expression.
Entity pages
Actors, tenants, executors and records get their own pages and timelines.
Operation trees
The events that share an operation id, from the request to the jobs it spawned.
Exports
CSV, JSON Lines, and an evidence export with every event’s original payload.
Retention you choose
Per project, from 7 days to 10 years. Expired events are permanently deleted.
Workspace activity
Logbird's own record of what your team does: projects, retention, keys, members, exports and sign-ins.
Teams and roles
Invitations, and owner, admin and member roles.
Integrations
Hono, TanStack Start, Vue and Nuxt.
Customer-facing history
Tenant-scoped read keys, and short-lived viewer tokens for the browser.
On the roadmap
What's on our roadmap.
These are candidates from our public roadmap, not features or commitments. Order and timing can change.
Project-level redaction controls, behind the redaction your client already does.
Alerts on chosen actions.
Signed webhooks and archive exports.
Single sign-on for the console.
Questions
Pricing questions.
Does self-hosting include every feature?
Yes. Once the source opens, everything in the repository is MIT-licensed, and self-hosting gets all of it.
How much will Logbird Cloud cost?
It's free during early access. Pricing after that isn't set yet; we'll publish it before billing starts.
How do I get early access?
Request access through the contact form. Sign-ups are limited to invited teams for now.
