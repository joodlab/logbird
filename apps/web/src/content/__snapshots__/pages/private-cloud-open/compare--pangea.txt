Logbird and Pangea Secure Audit Log
Pangea Secure Audit Log is a managed service built around tamper evidence. Every event goes into a Merkle tree. Pangea publishes the tree's root hashes to Arweave, a public, append-only ledger, and every search result can come with a proof that it hasn't been altered. Events can also be signed. Logs move through hot, warm and cold storage, and an embeddable React viewer lets your customers search and verify their logs. If you need to prove that logs weren't changed, that's what it was built for, and Logbird doesn't do it. [P1, P2, P3, P4]
Ownership, as of 7 November 2026
As of 7 November 2026: CrowdStrike completed its acquisition of Pangea on 26 September 2025, and Pangea's product and signup pages redirect to CrowdStrike, while the Secure Audit Log documentation and API are still published. Check current availability with CrowdStrike. [P5, P6, P7]

When to choose Pangea Secure Audit Log
You need cryptographic proof that audit events haven't been changed, with verifiable published roots.
You want event signing with managed or imported keys.
You want a ready-made, embeddable viewer that also verifies integrity.
You want tiered storage for very long retention and don't need to self-host.
When to choose Logbird
Your events must stay on your own servers, under an MIT licence.
You want to send any shape without designing a fixed schema first.
Your team investigates with an operator console: entity pages, operation trees, workers and retries, and SQL.
You want SQL-level questions answered against your own ClickHouse, under documented limits.
