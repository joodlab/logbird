About
We built the audit log we needed.
Logbird started as the audit log a product team needed, where every support question begins the same way: who changed this, and when? We wanted the answer to take one search, on servers we run ourselves. It's MIT-licensed, and the source opens publicly soon, so any product team can do the same.
Take the product tour
Contact us
First design partner
Shaped by a real product.
Logbird is being integrated with a hotel-booking SaaS, our first design partner. Integration feedback helps inform the roadmap.
What we hold to
Five decisions that don't change.
[aria-hidden] 01
Require only an action.
An action string is the only required field, and each event must fit the ingest limits. We recommend a shape; we never reject an event for missing it.
[aria-hidden] 02
Capture is your code's job.
We give you the SDK, the integrations and the patterns. Our guarantee starts at the 202.
[aria-hidden] 03
Your code sends the history.
Logbird doesn't rebuild history from your database. Import past events by sending them with their original occurredAt, up to 3650 days back; an older value is stored at receive time with a warning, and project retention still applies.
[aria-hidden] 04
Retention means deletion.
Expired events are deleted for good.
[aria-hidden] 05
We audit ourselves.
Logbird records its own management actions in your workspace's activity stream.
The name
Logbird
The bird that carries every event home. We chose a short name made of two words you already know, so anyone who hears it once can spell it.
Building in public
Follow the build.
We write up what we ship as we go. Every post uses real captures from the product.
